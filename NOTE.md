# What I Would Build Next for Our Dealers

If I joined PURE Energy, I would build a unified Dealer Operations Platform connecting lead management, customer follow-ups, quotations, service requests, and sales performance in one place.

As a Founding Engineer at Bandiwala, a Hyderabad-based startup with 1,800+ registered users, I gained hands-on experience building and maintaining a live product ecosystem. I contributed to integrating four applications—User, Vendor, Admin, and operational workflows—through a shared Firebase backend. This experience strengthened my understanding of Firebase, multi-role applications, data synchronization, and real-world business operations.

**Live application:** [Bandiwala User App — Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bandiwala.user)

I would apply these learnings to extend PURE Energy's Dealer Lead Router into a cross-platform application using Flutter and Firebase. Dealers could receive leads, schedule follow-ups, generate quotations, and track conversions, while management could monitor dealer performance through centralized dashboards and BigQuery analytics.

My goal would be to ship practical features quickly, gather feedback from dealers, and continuously improve reliability, accountability, and sales visibility as the network grows.
